Activity 8: Text Styling

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
  <head>
    <title>Activity 8</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <p>Ano ang meaning ng <i>glamorous</i> ba?</p>
    <p>Is it <u>True</u> or <u>False</u></p>

    <p>You are now <strike>DEAD</strike></p>

</html>
